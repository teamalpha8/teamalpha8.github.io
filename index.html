// 01 — Mission Overview

Why We Build This

The Problem

Existing military sleeping systems suffer critical field limitations: bulkiness of 3.2–3.5 kg, poor climate adaptability, hygiene degradation under sustained use, and restricted mobility during rapid deployment scenarios. Soldiers operating in Arctic, Himalayan, desert, and tropical theatres need a system that works across all environments without compromise.


ThermoShield Pro directly addresses soldier survivability, operational readiness, and logistical efficiency. The system achieves a 54% weight reduction below the 3.5 kg industry threshold while delivering a −50°C to +40°C operational range through purely passive thermoregulation — zero electronic dependency, zero failure modes.

Our Goals

  • ► Design a modular 5-layer sleeping system with each layer serving a distinct, non-redundant thermal or structural function
  • ► Achieve total system weight of ~1.59 kg — using NASA/ISRO-derived MLI as the primary insulation technology
  • ► Deliver passive thermoregulation across −50°C to +40°C without any batteries or electronics
  • ► Reduce thermal signature for stealth field compatibility
  • ► Enable full pack-down in under 45 seconds into 28 × 18 cm — fitting standard military daypack side pockets
  • ► Build for 5–8 year field lifespan under daily military use
  • ► Allow independent layer replacement and climate adaptation in-field
// 02 — System Architecture

5-Layer Thermal Design

01

Outer Shell — Weather Armour

20D Ripstop Nylon with DWR coating + TPU laminate inner face + metalized low-emissivity inner coating. Rated 10,000mm hydrostatic head pressure. Ripstop grid arrests tear propagation on rocky terrain. IR-suppressive dye option for stealth compatibility.

280g
02

MLI Core — Primary Innovation

10 alternating layers of 12-micron aluminized Mylar film and 0.5mm polyester mesh spacer. Reflects 97% of radiated body heat. Total thickness 3–4mm, weight 80–100 g/m². Heat-sealed construction — no needle penetration to preserve reflective integrity. Bridges a thermal gap of 68–74°C between environment and body surface.

200g
03

PCM Thermal Regulation Layer

Phase Change Material microcapsule-embedded textile placed at torso and foot zones only. Paraffin-based PCM with melting point 28–34°C absorbs excess body heat when warm and releases it when cooling — fully passive thermoregulation with no batteries or failure modes.

150g
04

Ground Resistance Panel — Bottom Only

5mm XPS foam bonded with 5 MLI layers specifically for the bottom-contact panel. XPS maintains R-value under 80kg body-weight compression — unlike pure MLI which loses air gaps under pressure. Detaches via perimeter zipper for use as standalone sit pad or ground mat — dual-purpose field utility.

180g
05

Inner Comfort Liner — Skin Contact

Bamboo-viscose and Merino wool blend. Naturally antimicrobial, moisture-wicking, and soft for extended multi-day field use without requiring base layers. Actively wicks sweat away from skin surface, preventing moisture buildup in colder conditions.

200g
MOISTURE CONTROL

ePTFE breathable membrane between MLI and inner liner allows sweat vapor to escape outward while blocking liquid water and ice crystal formation inward — critical below −35°C.

ERGONOMIC SHAPE

Mummy-contour hybrid: 85cm shoulder width tapering to 55cm at foot. Anatomically shaped trapezoidal foot box. Draft collar behind main zipper. Fully unzippable as flat quilt — dual operational mode.

QUICK DEPLOYMENT

Dual-sided YKK Aquaguard anti-snag zipper operable with one gloved hand. Single-pull hood cinch cord. 4-compression strap stuff sack — full pack-down in under 45 seconds.
