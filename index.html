Development of an efficient, modular, lightweight, and climate-adaptive military sleeping system to enhance soldier comfort, mobility, and operational readiness in extreme field conditions. [cite: 3]

Our Focus & Goal

The Problem

Existing systems face severe limitations including bulkiness (3.2–3.5 kg), poor climate adaptability, hygiene degradation in field conditions, and restricted mobility during rapid deployment scenarios. [cite: 6]

The Solution

A portable thermal sleeping bag engineered specifically for defence personnel, high-altitude operatives, and extreme environment hikers. [cite: 7] It features an operational temperature range of −50°C to +40°C [cite: 10] with a total system weight of ~1.59 kg — 54% below the 3.5 kg industry threshold. [cite: 9] The system uses passive thermoregulation with zero electronic dependency. [cite: 11]

Materials & Architecture

The ThermoShield Pro is built on a modular 5-layer architecture. [cite: 18] Below are the critical components and materials utilized in our prototype: [cite: 70]

Outer Fabric

20D Ripstop Nylon, DWR coated, TPU laminate inner face. Weather resistant and IR suppressive. [cite: 70]

MLI Core

12-micron, aluminium vapour deposited Mylar Film (97% reflectivity) [cite: 70] with a 0.5mm Polyester Mesh Spacer. [cite: 70]

Thermal Regulation

Paraffin microcapsule embedded PCM Textile (melting point 28–34°C) positioned in the torso and foot zones. [cite: 70]

Ground Resistance

5mm thickness XPS Foam Sheet that is compression resistant and R-value stable for the bottom panel. [cite: 70]

Moisture Control

Gore-Tex class ePTFE Membrane, acting as a breathable waterproof layer. [cite: 70]

Inner Lining

Bamboo viscose + Merino wool blend acting as an antimicrobial comfort liner. [cite: 70]

Hardware

Construction
