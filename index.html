ThermoShield Pro

Developed by Team 8

A modular, lightweight, and climate-adaptive military sleeping system designed for extreme field conditions.

Our Focus & Goal

The Challenge

Current military systems are often bulky (3.5kg) and struggle with hygiene and extreme cold. Our focus was to reduce this weight significantly while expanding the operational temperature range.

The Goal

We engineered the ThermoShield Pro to weigh just 1.59 kg—a 54% reduction. It is designed to function from −50°C to +40°C using passive thermoregulation, ensuring soldier survival in Arctic, desert, or mountain environments.

Modular Architecture

Outer Shell

20D Ripstop Nylon with DWR coating and TPU laminate. Provides waterproof and IR-suppressive protection.

MLI Core

NASA/ISRO-derived Multilayer Reflective Insulation reflecting 97% of body heat using aluminized Mylar.

Active Regulation

Phase Change Material (PCM) microcapsules that absorb and release heat to maintain body temperature.

Ground Panel

XPS Foam bonded with MLI to maintain thermal resistance even under the weight of the body.

Breathable Membrane

ePTFE (Gore-Tex class) layer that prevents moisture buildup and freezing within the bag layers.

Comfort Liner

Bamboo-viscose and Merino wool blend for natural antimicrobial properties and moisture wicking.
